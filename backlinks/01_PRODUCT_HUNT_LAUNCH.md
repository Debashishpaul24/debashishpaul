## 2. Description (Short & Compelling)

```text
DineFlow™ is a high-performance, multi-tenant digital dining and kitchen operating system engineered to eliminate third-party aggregator commissions (15%–25%) and cut restaurant table turn times by 31%.

Key capabilities:
📱 Zero App-Install QR Ordering: Diners scan a table tent QR code to launch an instant PWA menu, customize spice levels/allergies, and order in seconds.
⚡ Real-Time Kitchen Display System (KDS): Orders fire directly to the kitchen line in <50ms with acoustic alerts and 5-stage cooking tracking.
💳 Dynamic Direct UPI Billing: Guests settle bills table-side directly into the restaurant’s bank account with 0% middleman fees.
🖨️ Thermal Receipt Printing: Native 80mm ESC/POS hardware engine for instant KOT tickets and GST tax invoices.
🎮 Live Dual-Device Sandbox: Test the guest mobile view and kitchen display side-by-side in your desktop browser.
```
