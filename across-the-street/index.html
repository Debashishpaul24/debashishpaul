THE HERITAGE & AMBIENCE

A Tranquil Cottage Sanctuary in the Heart of New Town

Nestled quietly near Candor Gate 1 in Action Area I, Across The Street was envisioned as a sanctuary from the urban rush—where warm amber illumination spills through floor-to-ceiling glass onto tranquil manicured lawns.

Whether you are dining beneath crystal chandeliers on the second floor or enjoying an evening breeze on the alfresco garden patio under vintage streetlamps, every corner invites you to unwind, converse, and celebrate over extraordinary food.

🍽️

Artisanal Slow Cooking

Hand-pounded spice blends, slow-simmered Awadhi gravies, and fire-baked Italian thin-crust pizzas crafted from scratch.

Alfresco Garden Romance
